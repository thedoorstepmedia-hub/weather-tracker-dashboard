    How these numbers are calculated

    Risk scores and auto/property pressure indices are a transparent, documented heuristic (alert severity × a regional exposure proxy × a generic auto/property loss-pattern split) — not an official forecast. Claims-timing phases and the suggested checklist use a generic published P&C pattern, not Intact-specific data. Historical context (percentile, similar events, seasonal baseline, records) is computed purely from this tracker's own accumulated public weather history — no company data is used or stored anywhere in this tool, including the private source repo.

    Data source: Environment Canada MSC GeoMet weather-alerts API. Refreshed every 15 minutes.
